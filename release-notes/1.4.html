Export Captions Only

  • Export Captions Only (Transparent) writes just the animated captions over a transparent background, as “<name> Captions.mov”, to lay over your video in Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or any editor that reads alpha. It is in the File menu (⌥⌘E), the arrow beside the Export button and the queue's right-click menu on the Mac, and beside Export in the editor on iPhone and iPad.
  • The file has the same size, frame rate and length as the original, frame for frame, so it lines up when you drop it on the timeline. It has no audio and leaves the watermark out.
  • ProRes 4444 by default. Settings › Export › Captions-only format switches to HEVC with transparency for much smaller files; that format is also used on a device that can't write ProRes 4444.
  • Watched folders skip “… Captions.mov” files, as they do “… Captioned” ones.
