Timeline

  • A timeline runs across the bottom of the editor: the captions as blocks over a filmstrip of the video, with the playhead through both. Drag a block to move a caption, or drag either edge to change how long it stays on screen; blocks stop at their neighbours and snap to them and to the playhead, and the words inside keep their timing in proportion.
  • Click a block to select it in the list and jump there; right-click (long-press on iPhone and iPad) to edit its timing or delete it. Drag the ruler to scrub.
  • Zoom with ⌘-scroll or the −/+ slider on the Mac (pinch on iPhone and iPad). Hide and show the timeline from the toolbar or the View menu (⌥⌘T), and drag the handle above it to change its height.
